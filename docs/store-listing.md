## Short Description

Learn Mandarin with HSK 1-3 flashcards, real-life missions, smart review, pronunciation, quizzes, and an optional AI tutor.

## Full Description

HanYu Cards helps adult learners build Mandarin vocabulary from HSK levels 1 to 3 and use it in realistic situations. Study with focused flashcards, hear pronunciation through device speech synthesis, review words on a spaced schedule, and test recall with quick multiple-choice practice.

Six task-based missions cover meeting someone new, ordering a meal, buying clothes, taking the metro, planning a campus project, and visiting a clinic. Each mission combines useful expressions, bilingual dialogue, culture in context, and a response task so learners move from recognition to practical communication.

The app is offline-first: your vocabulary progress stays on your device and no account is required. The optional AI tutor can explain the current word, create mnemonics, and quiz recent mistakes when connected to the publisher's assistant service.

Key features:

- Complete built-in HSK 1-3 vocabulary set.
- Simplified and optional traditional character display.
- Pinyin, English meanings, radicals, tags, and review status.
- Smart review buttons: Again, Hard, Good, Easy.
- Six real-life missions with dialogue, pronunciation, culture notes, and applied tasks.
- Dictionary search by Chinese, pinyin, or English.
- Daily goal, accuracy, due count, weekly activity, mission completion, and level progress.
- Optional AI tutor with scenario role-play and personalized study plans.
- No ads and no tracking in the default release.
