HanYu Cards Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 16, 2026

HanYu Cards is an offline-first Mandarin learning app. Vocabulary progress, review history, daily goal, and settings are stored locally on your device.

Data We Collect

The app does not require an account and does not sell personal data. When you send a message to the AI tutor, the app sends your message, the current vocabulary word, due word count, mastered count, and recent mistake words to the configured assistant API so it can answer.

Data Storage

Learning progress is stored in device browser storage or the native app webview storage. You can reset local progress in Settings.

Third Parties

If the AI tutor is enabled, messages are processed by the configured AI provider through the app backend. Review your deployment provider's terms before publishing.
